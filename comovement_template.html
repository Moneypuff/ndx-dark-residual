02

How it's built

No look-ahead in the outcome: each regime is scored on the day it's observed, by the return realised over the following 21 sessions.

DIX
Dollar-weighted Σ($ short) / Σ($ off-exchange) across each index's constituents — SqueezeMetrics' construction. High DIX is the conventionally bullish read (market-makers shorting to fill dark buy orders = accumulation).
DIX5
The 5-day moving average of DIX — the same smoothing the dashboard uses as its residual benchmark.
Regime
Per index, DIX5 bucketed by decile over the common sample: Low = deciles 1–3, Mid = 4–7, High = 8–10. Three indices → 27 possible comovement regimes.
Return
Each index's own price proxy 21 trading days ahead, in percent — QQQ for NDX, SPY for SPX, IWM for the Russell 2000.

06

What the pattern says

Four reads that hold across the table.

◆

Divergence carries the signal, not the level

All-hot and all-cold regimes both sit near baseline (+1.5% to +2.3%). The large positive and negative months cluster in the mixed regimes — the joint DIX level alone barely discriminates.

◆

A high IWM DIX, on its own, is a poor omen for small caps

The purest case — IWM DIX High while both large caps sit only Mid — turns small caps slightly negative (−0.20%, hit 46%) — a contrarian, mean-reverting tell.

◆

…but the Low/Low/High divergence flips it bullish

Pair that hot IWM DIX with Low SPX and NDX DIX and all three indices run above baseline (+2.2% to +2.9%), IWM lifting most vs. its own norm. The divergence, not the IWM level, is what matters.

◆

The cleanest large-cap tailwind is the opposite tilt

Large-cap DIX firm while small-cap DIX is Low produced the best months in the sample — NDX +6.79% at a 94% hit-rate when NDX High / SPX Mid / IWM Low.

07

Read with these in mind

Overlapping windows. 21-day forward returns overlap heavily, so ~1,648 sessions is only ~78 independent months. Treat medians and hit-rates as sturdier than means, and small buckets as suggestive.
Sample window. Starts Jan 2020, so the COVID crash-and-recovery and the 2022 drawdown both sit inside it and inflate some means and hit-rates.
In-sample deciles. Regime thresholds are full-history deciles of DIX5; live use would key off provisional, expanding-window cutoffs.
IWM DIX is reconstructed from iShares' Russell 2000 holdings and spans a wider raw range than the NDX/SPX gauges — its deciles are internally consistent, not comparable in level.
Path metrics are noisy per-day. A single day's forward volatility or efficiency ratio is one realization of a 21-day path; only the cell-level medians in the "How the month unfolds" section are meaningful, and small cells are suggestive at best. Full robustness detail — a vol-persistence control, expanding-window cutoffs, an out-of-sample split — is in REGIME_PATHS_FINDINGS.md.
