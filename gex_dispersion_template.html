Gamma × correlation → the tape's character

One dial for gamma, one for how much stocks move together.

Dealer gamma (SqueezeMetrics' GEX) sets how hard option hedging leans on the tape; 1-month correlation across S&P 500 stocks sets whether the tape moves as one trade or five hundred. 02

How it's built

No look-ahead in the outcome: each session is scored by the return and realized vol over the following 21 sessions.

GEX
SqueezeMetrics' public daily SPX gamma exposure ($ per 1% move) — the free ancestor of the gated GEX+ series. Positive = dealers long gamma, hedging against moves; deeply negative = hedging with them. Ranked as a percentile within its trailing year, since raw dollars drift upward with the options market.
SqueezeMetrics' GEX+ — their yacht-club successor to classic GEX, folding strike-level gamma and vanna (VEX) exposure into one dealer-hedging gauge (here rescaled to $ per 1% move). Positive = dealers hedging against moves; deeply negative = hedging with them. Ranked as a percentile within its trailing year, since raw dollars drift upward with the options market.
Correlation
Our own realized analog of Cboe's implied correlation: over the top-50 IVV names (weights renormalized), 21-session vols feed the basket identity ρ = (σ²_P − Σw²σ²) / ((Σwσ)² − Σw²σ²) — the average pairwise correlation implied by how much basket vol exceeds the sum of its parts. Quoted 0–100 like COR1M.
Dispersion
The same inputs recombined as √(Σwσ²_i − σ²_P), annualized vol points — how much single-stock movement the index didn't inherit. The realized cousin of Cboe's DSPX.
Component vol
The gross single-stock term alone, √(Σwσ²_i), annualized vol points — the square root of the weighted average constituent variance (≥ the average vol), before the index nets any of it out. The realized cousin of Cboe's VIXEQ (VIXEQ² ≈ VIX² + DSPX²).
Quadrant
Gamma dial High/Low at its rolling-year 50th percentile × correlation dial High/Low at its full-sample median. Four regimes; the hero card scores today's.
Outcome
SPX (SqueezeMetrics' close series) 21 trading days ahead: the % return, and the annualized realized vol of those 21 daily moves.

06

Two triangles, leg by leg

Cboe's implied triangle is VIXEQ² = VIX² + DSPX²; our realized one, cvol² = index vol² + dispersion², closes by construction. A triangle built entirely in one layer says nothing — the information is in laying the implied triangle over the realized one and reading each pair of legs as an implied-minus-realized premium: single-name vol (hypotenuses), index vol (adjacent legs), and correlation (the angles — here 100·(VIX/VIXEQ)², which tracks Cboe's COR1M at r ≈ 0.9 but isn't identical: COR1M uses the exact basket identity). Each premium is ranked against its own history to date (expanding percentile — no look-ahead, and any slow basis drift re-centers itself) and bucketed into quintiles: Q1 = cheapest fifth so far, Q5 = richest (fear). The ◆ row is today. The chart pane's tricor = 100·(VIX/cvol)² is a hybrid cousin of the adjacent-leg premium — an implied-over-realized comovement ratio, not the correlation the option market pays — and is clipped at 100 on the handful of crash onsets where VIX exceeds cvol.

−3%+3%

Statistics, honestly: 21-day outcomes overlap, so each bin's days collapse to the ~months shown; a quintile's mean then carries a standard error of roughly 4.5 / √months ≈ 0.7pp, and most spreads in these tables sit within one standard error of each other — read them as description, not proof. Levels also embed a permanent basis (top-50 vs full index, 21 trading vs 30 calendar days); the percentile ranking absorbs it.

08

Read with these in mind

Classic GEX, not GEX+. The public series assumes calls are dealer-long and puts dealer-short at every strike; SqueezeMetrics' gated GEX+ relaxes that. Treat the sign near zero as soft.
GEX+ is a model. SqueezeMetrics' dealer-positioning fit is proprietary; the series is consumed as published (GEX plus VEX under their assumptions) and ranked, not re-derived — treat the dollar level as their estimate, the percentile as ours.
Today's basket, yesterday's tape. The realized gauges use the current top-50 IVV names and weights across all of history — survivorship and weight drift grow with lookback. Cboe's COR1M is the point-in-time clean version; that's why it's on the chart.
Implied ≠ realized. COR1M and DSPX price the coming month with a risk premium; our gauges measure the month just passed. Levels differ by construction — judge the comovement, not the gap.
The triangle mixes clocks. tricor divides a forward implied vol (VIX) by a trailing realized one (cvol), so its gap over realized correlation bundles the correlation premium with the index vol premium — a fear gauge, not a pure correlation forecast.
Overlapping windows. 21-day outcomes overlap heavily; — sessions is only ~— independent months. Medians and hit-rates over means.
In-sample midpoint. The correlation dial's High/Low cut is the full-sample median — live use would key off an expanding cutoff. The gamma dial's trailing-year rank has no such look-ahead.
Quadrants are coarse. A 50/50 cross treats a 51st-percentile day like a 99th. The map above shows the full continuum; the table is deliberately blunt.
