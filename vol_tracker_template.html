Vol tracker & options trade log

The regime log's live signals expressed in options: structures picked from the signal-conditional expected move and the smile's skew, marked off the despiked live surface (never raw closing quotes), entered and exited by the playbook's fixed rules. Liquid chains only — the gate below is recomputed nightly.

Trade log

deterministic replay: enter on the first snapshot at/after each signal, exit at event + 63 sessions · marks in % of spot, debit positive · smile-marked, quote-bounded only when the market is tight · illiquid chains get the delta-1 stock trade: stop at the family's q25 excursion, take-profit at its median max-favorable, sized so the stop costs 1% of NAV

Risk reversals (delta pillars, constant maturity)

our own Black-Scholes IV and delta at the 10Δ/25Δ/50Δ pillars, interpolated to fixed 30d/90d maturities · RR = call-wing IV − put-wing IV at the same delta, in vol points (negative = puts bid) · deep history from ORATS, extended daily from the live capture
