03

The playbook

Situation → action, from the usage guide. The dials size risk and manage themes; they do not call index direction.

Gamma releases 40+ pts in a month
Rotation window open. Tighten stops on crowded themes, trim relative-value gross. This is the warning, not the signal.
Theme breaks · no shock nearby
Exit or trim. Expected value of holding ≈ zero from here. With the gamma tag too — especially on simultaneous multi-theme breaks — lean against re-entry.
Theme breaks · correlation shock just hit
Sit on your hands. Historically the theme going on sale, not ending.
DSPX unwinding · realized dispersion still high
Audit crowded relative-value before the unwind finds it — the street is taking the dispersion trade down.
Tape re-pinned after a rotation
New leadership settles. Fresh breaks without the gamma tag get de-risk weight, not conviction.
04

Read with these in mind

Not a direction machine. The quadrant forecasts volatility, not returns; the rotation bucket is worth ~2pp/quarter at p≈0.1. Use for sizing and exits, never as a standalone short signal.
Fixed thresholds. z ±1.5, 40-pt gamma drop, ±5pp theme cut — chosen on a robust plateau, but in-sample. The bucket stats are frozen backtest numbers, not live-updating estimates.
Leaderboard ≠ signals. Sector-vs-SPY spreads are structurally tamer than pair spreads; HOT/UNFAVORED states are a screen for candidates you'd hold for independent reasons.
Signals are close-to-close. Everything computes off daily closes with a 63-session cooldown per pair; nothing here is intraday.
