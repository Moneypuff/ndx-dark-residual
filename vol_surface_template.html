Vol surface

Every captured expiry's live, despiked smile resampled onto a fixed 60%–140% moneyness grid and stacked by time to expiry — no extrapolation, so a grid cell outside a smile's own strike range is left as an honest hole rather than a flat-carried edge. Drag to rotate, scrub the date to replay the capture history.
